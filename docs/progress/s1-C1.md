## How to run

```
cd apps/web
corepack pnpm dev          # predev copies the Cesium static files
# http://localhost:3000/dev/cesium          tier from env (dev default B)
# http://localhost:3000/dev/cesium?tier=A   Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles (spends one ion Google session per load)
# http://localhost:3000/dev/cesium?tier=C   keyless, offline
```

Env: `NEXT_PUBLIC_CESIUM_ION_TOKEN` (empty -> tier C only, no ion calls), `NEXT_PUBLIC_TILE_TIER=A|B|C` (optional). Default start tier: B in development, A in production, falling back downward.

Tier B therefore tries `createWorldImageryAsync()` and falls back to `IonImageryProvider.fromAssetId(3830182)` ("Google Maps 2D Satellite", id from that list). The comment in `components/globe/types.ts` should be updated by whoever owns the contract; I did not change it.

Fallback B -> C was also seen working (while the token file was still broken, ion returned 401 and the page reported tier C).

- Stars + atmosphere, no Cesium widgets.
- Auto-rotate: camera longitude moved about 2 degrees per second; stopped after the first click. Hover does not stop it, by design (pointer down, wheel and key do).
- Pins: twin = large teal diamond with a dot, explore = small white disc. Hover showed the label "name, country" and a pointer cursor. Click selected the pin; click on empty space cleared it. Picking a pin worked against the globe (B, C) and against the Google tileset (A). Pins on the far side of the Earth are hidden.
- Selecting in globe view flew to the pin at 7,000 km height. Farm view ended at 1,061 m above ground, pitch -45 degrees, heading 0.
- Clamp (tier C, radius 1500 m): camera teleported to 4,879 m out and 7,876 m up eased to 2,726 m / 4,770 m after 150 ms and settled at 1,500 m / 3,000 m. Camera put 2 m above ground rose to 20 m. In tier A a camera put below ground rose to 20 m above the tiles.
- Homepage (`/`, agent-shell's `GlobeHome`): globe rendered at tier B with 8 pins, one canvas, no page errors.

Not checked: `reducedMotion` paths (code only), touch input, WebGL-unavailable and context-loss paths (code only), strict-mode double mount beyond "one canvas on the page, no errors".

## Drape + split results

| Method | Globe terrain (B, C) | Google 3D tiles (A) | Split | Per-frame update |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (a) `GroundPrimitive`, `ClassificationType.BOTH`, canvas-backed material | Works | Works | Works (in the material shader, see below) | Smooth |
| (b) `SingleTileImageryProvider` layer on `viewer.imageryLayers` + `splitDirection` | Works when still | Not applicable (globe hidden) | Works when still | Broken while playing: one side went missing and the other showed a stale frame |
| (c) same layer on `tileset.imageryLayers` | Not applicable | Nothing drew in my test | Not reached | Not reached |

**Recommended: method (a) for every tier.** One code path, works on tiles and on terrain.

- Split: `GroundPrimitive` has no `splitDirection`, so the drape material discards fragments on the wrong side of `czm_splitPosition`, the same test Cesium's own shaders use. The page sets `scene.splitPosition` (0..1). Two drapes (left/right), one viewer, one camera: tilting and orbiting moved both sides together. This is a real shared camera, not two synced viewers.
- Texture update: Cesium's `Material` only re-uploads when the image uniform is a different object, so the drape alternates two 256x256 canvases.
- Cesium's docs say textured ground primitives "are not meant for precisely mapping textures to terrain". On the test pattern the drape followed terrain and buildings and sat where expected, but cell-accurate registration against a real DEM has not been checked.
- On Google tiles the drape also paints roofs and tree tops (it classifies everything under the rectangle).

## Performance (one machine, not a benchmark)

Intel Arc 140T, 1440x900 viewport, dev build, split on, two 256x256 drapes updated every animation frame, farm view: about 109 rendered frames/s in C, 99 to 120 in B, 72 in A. The imagery method ran at about 55 in C and was visually wrong while playing. Idle scenes do not render (`requestRenderMode`).

## Known gaps

- The contract has no way to hand drape data or the viewer to the product UI. `GlobeDev`'s `onViewer` does it for the dev page; C3/C4 need a contract decision (extend `GlobeProps`, or a second component).
- Globe view has no extra pin label styling for small screens; keyboard users rely on the shell's farm list (the canvas is not keyboard-operable).
- In tier A `tileset.getHeight` returned undefined at one camera position; the clamp then uses the farm centre's terrain height, which is fine on flat farms and approximate on steep ones.
- Rapid wheel zoom at the 20 m floor can throw the camera sideways before the clamp pulls it back.
- Pins are rebuilt when the selection changes (fine for tens of pins).
- Imagery drape method is left in `drape.ts` for comparison only; delete it once (a) is adopted.
- Playwright's console log records ion request URLs, which contain the token. I deleted my logs and screenshots; anyone re-running browser checks should do the same.
